Purpose and claim hierarchy
The user's broader hypothesis is that curriculum helps a transformer acquire
ICL faster or more reliably. The PRIMARY test compares joint dimension/context
curriculum with independently tuned full-difficulty training from the start.
This compares training policies, including exposure to easier tasks. A GATED
SECONDARY test compares curriculum with exactly exposure-matched shuffled
training, isolating ordering with a common optimizer. Both use clean-target
learning-curve AUC. This is not a test of the original increasing-noise-order
robustness hypothesis, which remains unsupported. New experiments are corrected
all-eligible-prefix, 50D synthetic regression, not exact historical replication.
The previous two-seed screens and five-seed discovery informed this design.
This is a locally frozen prospective confirmation, not an externally registered
study, proof of a universal claim, mechanism test, or pretrained-LLM experiment.

Fixed execution: 96 production models, 15 GPUs maximum in either array
Development: seeds 600,601,602 x four optimizer candidates x three arms = 36
models, 50,000 updates each. Confirmation: seeds 1000..1019 inclusive x three
arms = 60 models, 125,000 updates each. Development and confirmation seeds and
evaluation banks are disjoint and checked against prior run manifests/metrics.
All models are clean trained. Report every run, including stalls and nulls.
No other task/architecture search, additional seeds, or optional stopping is
authorized by this protocol. A separate mechanism or generalization study can
be designed later; it is not necessary to interpret the scoped primary result.

Shared architecture/data/update rules
Reuse unchanged verified run_experiment.py and robustness_experiment.py, with
a thin adapter changing only provenance and cohort configuration. GPT-2 causal
regression transformer: 12 layers, embedding 256, eight heads, zero dropout,
eager attention, no cache. Physical shapes: 50 coordinates, 101 prediction
positions, 202 interleaved tokens throughout every optimizer update. AdamW
weight decay .01, gradient clip 1, effective batch 256, microbatch 128.
BF16 autocast forward/backward, FP32 parameters and FP32 loss, TF32 disabled,
deterministic algorithms. Fresh Gaussian task vectors/inputs use stateless
seed/batch-ID/stream RNG. Within each seed all models start with exactly the
same parameters; seed/config/source/protocol/evaluation identity is preserved.

Canonical schedule and target-scale control
IDs 0..999: scheduled dimension 5. IDs 1000..24999: dimension
5+floor(45*(ID-1000)/23999). IDs >=25000: full 50D.
Curriculum visits ascending IDs with scheduled dimension d and 2d+1 eligible
loss-prefix positions. Shuffled uses a seed-fixed permutation of the identical
first 25k IDs, followed by the same ascending IDs for the remaining updates.
Thus their exact realized batches/targets/loss-mask/label-scale multiset match.
Hard-only uses full 50D and all 101 eligible positions at every ascending ID.
For all arms labels/targets are sqrt(50/active_dimension)*(x dot w), with inputs
masked to active dimensions. Expected target variance is therefore always 50.
All arms share identical realized full-difficulty batches and chronology after
update 25k. Training minimizes clean MSE at every eligible x token, including
k=0. Query prediction cannot observe its own target through causal attention.

Development optimizer grid and selection (fixed before new outcomes)
In this exact constant-variance distribution, candidates in fixed tie order:
1. lr_low: LR .0001, raw eligible-prefix MSE.
2. baseline: LR .0004, raw MSE.
3. lr_high: LR .001, raw MSE.
4. loss_nmse: LR .0004, raw MSE divided by active_dimension before backward.
All candidates use BF16 and identical settings otherwise. The divided-loss
candidate changes weighting/gradient clipping; it does not rescale input labels.
Equal search budget for each arm: four candidates x three seeds x 50k updates.
Rank by mean across development seeds of clean NMSE trapezoidally averaged at
25k,30k,40k,50k updates, lower is better. Exact ties use the listed order.
Select the SHUFFLED winner as the COMMON optimizer for both confirmation
curriculum and shuffled. Independently select the HARD winner for confirmation
hard-only. Publish curriculum's development ranking too, but do not use it to
select a different curriculum optimizer. This intentionally gives the baselines
a strong chance to learn. The primary is a conservative policy comparison,
not an optimizer-matched ordering contrast; the secondary is optimizer-matched.
These are best candidates within this limited prespecified grid, not globally
optimal settings. Previous raw-label optimizer rankings do not select settings.
Only the development primary-bank clean AUC enters selection. No holdout or
confirmation result may enter tuning. Missing/nonfinite runs halt selection;
finite stalled runs remain eligible and are ranked at their observed error.
All development results are exploratory selection data, never confirmation.

Confirmation outcomes and statistics
20 fresh paired training seeds, three models per seed, fixed budget 125k.
Evaluation times: 0,1k,5k,10k,20k,25k,30k,40k,50k,75k,100k,125k updates.
All primary evaluations use a shared new full-50D bank seed 731841 with 4096
episodes. Clean-target squared error is divided by 50 and averaged over query
contexts k=50..100 inclusive. PRIMARY outcome per model is the trapezoidal
mean of this clean NMSE over 25k..125k; divide by 100k to retain NMSE units.
PRIMARY paired contrast: curriculum AUC minus tuned-hard AUC, negative better.
Primary support requires ALL: two-sided exact sign-test p<.05 for paired seed
differences; observed mean AUC reduction >=.02; and curriculum final mean clean
NMSE <=.25. The sign test concerns probability of a seed win, not mean effect.
Numerical ties abs(difference)<=1e-8 are excluded from sign-test n and reported.
Show all 20 differences, mean, median, win rate and a descriptive paired-seed
bootstrap percentile mean interval: 10,000 resamples, RNG seed 845731.
Do not treat that interval as the primary significance test or extra seeds.
Without ties 15/20 wins gives two-sided p=.041389. Assuming true seed-win
probability .8, probability of at least 15 wins is .8042. This is an illustrative
sample-size rationale, not a prediction, assurance, or power guarantee for the
practical-effect/final-accuracy gates.

Fixed-sequence confirmatory hierarchy for two claims
Only if the primary FULL support gate passes can the same .05 sign-test and
practical/final gates support a confirmatory ordering claim, using paired
curriculum-minus-shuffled AUC. This fixed sequence controls familywise error
for the two claims; no separate uncorrected parallel significance search.
If the primary fails, show ordering results and its p-value descriptively, but
do not substitute that secondary success for failed primary confirmation.
If primary passes and ordering fails, conclude easier-task curriculum policy
helps here, with no confirmed additional benefit of ascending chronology.
If both pass, conclude curriculum policy helps and ordering adds benefit here.
If primary fails, report failure to confirm the prespecified useful benefit;
do not assert universal absence from this finite test.

Other outcomes are SECONDARY/DESCRIPTIVE, no extra confirmatory claims
Final clean NMSE, learned counts at final NMSE<=.25, full error-versus-context
curves, scheduled time to .25 (interval-/right-censored; no interpolation),
training/GPU/wall time and Gaussian context-label noise SD1/SD2 with clean
query targets. A separate final holdout bank 731842 (4096 tasks) evaluates
unchanged final weights. Fresh tasks are not new independent training seeds.
Development uses banks 731839 primary and 731840 final holdout, also 4096 tasks.
The heldout bank cannot select a model, stopping point or optimizer.

Integrity, preservation, job dependencies and failure handling
CPU scientific checks, fresh-seed/bank audit and production batch probes must
pass, followed by production-shape GPU tests of finite updates, initialization,
RNG isolation, causal/query-target-absent predictions and bitwise resumed vs
uninterrupted training across all four optimizer candidates and all three arms.
Every stage checks the pre-submission source freeze. Slurm dependencies:
CPU preflight -> GPU smoke -> 36-model development array -> strict CPU selector
-> 60-model confirmation array -> strict CPU summary. All edges afterok;
invalid/failed dependencies cannot launch subsequent stages. Each array capped
at 15 A100 80GB GPUs, account share-ie-idi, QoS high. No overlapping arrays.
Rolling model/Adam/RNG checkpoints every 1000 updates; frozen checkpoints at
every evaluation. Technical interruption resumes SAME seed/config/source/
schedule from saved state to SAME budget. No dropping failed/stalled runs.
Sources/tasks/protocol are SHA256-frozen before preflight. A deterministic
selector writes a locked decision and resolved confirmation tasks after strict
verification of all 36 development models. Their hashes enter every
confirmation identity. Sources cannot change when the optimizer is selected.
The final summary refuses incomplete runs, wrong schedules or identities,
different weights for holdouts, inconsistent paired initialization, changed
optimizer/update counts, nonfinite metrics, or mismatched curves/logs. Preserve
individual metrics and all full curves, hashes, runtime/package versions,
submission IDs and complete weights on IDUN. No messages to Srikar now.
