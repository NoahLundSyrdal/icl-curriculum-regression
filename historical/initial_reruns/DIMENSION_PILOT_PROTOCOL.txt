Curriculum ordering in harder in-context regression: bounded exploratory pilot
Fixed before submission, 6 October 2026.

Purpose
Test whether a curriculum over task dimension AND context length accelerates
learning of a harder 50-dimensional linear-regression task. This is a new
question after the completed, inconclusive noise-ordering study. Broad curriculum
learning is within the thesis scope. Positive results are hypotheses, not a
requirement. The existing H1/H2 reports remain unchanged.

Rationale and limits
Garg et al., arXiv:2208.01066, Section 6/Appendix B.5, show substantial benefits
from dimension/context curricula, particularly at higher dimensions. A simple
replication alone would add limited novelty. This pilot adds an exact matched-
batch shuffled comparison and a common terminal phase to distinguish ordering
from exposure to easier tasks. We jointly change dimension/context length and
cannot attribute any effect uniquely to either component.

Arms and exact data matching
Two fresh initialization seeds: 100 and 101. Three arms per seed:
curriculum, shuffled, hard. Each runs 6,000 optimizer updates from scratch.
Define a fixed canonical bank of training BATCH IDs 0..5999. For IDs 0..999,
active dimension is 5. For IDs 1000..2999, active dimension rises monotonically
from 5 to 50 using floor(45*(ID-1000)/1999)+5. For IDs 3000..5999 it is 50.
The eligible prediction positions per batch are 2*active_dimension+1.
curriculum processes these IDs in order. shuffled processes one seeded random
permutation of IDs 0..2999, then IDs 3000..5999 in the same order as curriculum.
hard uses IDs in order, always with all 50 dimensions and 101 positions.
All arms therefore have a common final 3,000-update phase with exactly identical
batches. Curriculum and shuffled see exactly the SAME full training batches,
targets, active dimensions and eligible positions, with only chronology changed.
The hard arm shares underlying full-dimensional w/x episodes but changes the
initial 3,000 batches to full difficulty.

Stateless sampling and model
CPU Gaussian generators derive independent task/input/corruption seeds by SHA256
of version, initialization seed, batch ID and stream name. They have no dependency
on optimizer step or ordering. Draw the complete effective batch before slicing
microbatches. Generate w~N(0,I_50), x~N(0,I_50), then zero coordinates beyond
the batch's active dimension and compute clean y=xw. Training context labels are
clean in ALL arms: this isolates task-difficulty curriculum rather than exposure
to noise. Gaussian test corruption SD=1,2 is secondary.
Use the unchanged RegressionTransformer architecture from run_experiment.py:
12 layers, width 256, 8 heads, dropout 0, positional capacity 101 x positions.
AdamW lr=4e-4, weight decay=.01, norm clipping=1, BF16 GPU operations and FP32
raw squared-error loss, averaged over eligible prediction positions. Effective
batch=256, microbatch=128. No learning-rate schedule or early stopping.
The model physically processes all 101 x positions (202 tokens) in every arm
and every update; only the loss is restricted to eligible prefix positions.
Thus forward/dense-backward tensor shapes, total processed tokens and optimizer
update counts are matched. Report measured training time separately; equal
shapes are not a promise of identical hardware runtime. Context/target values
beyond the eligible prefix cannot affect earlier predictions causally.

Fixed evaluation and primary pilot contrast
New shared bank: eval seed 173819, 1,024 episodes, full dimension 50 and 101
prediction positions for ALL arms and ALL evaluation times. Gaussian label
noise SDs 0,1,2; query truth remains clean. NMSE=squared error/50, and k means
the number of preceding labels. Main target error averages k=50..100.
Measure at optimizer updates 0,500,1000,2000,3000,4000,5000,6000.
Primary PILOT contrast: curriculum minus shuffled, for the trapezoidal mean
of the SD=0 target error over the common full-difficulty phase, updates
3000..6000, with endpoints at 3000,4000,5000,6000. Negative favors curriculum.
Secondary: corresponding curriculum-hard contrast, final endpoint, full curves,
test SD1/SD2 robustness, per-prefix curves and measured training time.
Report both per-seed contrasts and their mean; TWO SEEDS ARE EXPLORATORY.
Do not claim confirmatory significance, practical equivalence or publishability.
The full-difficulty bank prevents comparing easier training losses as though
they were superior target-task learning. No outcome-driven early stopping.

Validation, resources and decision
Before production, verify exact batch identity under permutation, equal data
histograms and eligible-prefix totals, identical terminal batches, fixed full-
difficulty evaluation, causal independence from future tokens/labels, matched
initialization, evaluation RNG isolation, and bitwise tiny CPU interruption/
resume against uninterrupted training. Reject altered source/protocol on resume.
Preflight runs on Slurm CPUQ. One separate GPU smoke job checks production shapes,
BF16 training and curriculum/shuffled identity at baseline. Its directory is
separate from the pilot. Six GPU array tasks may run in parallel on A100 80GB.
Automatic summary depends on all six succeeding and requires all 48 evaluations.
Retain model/Adam/RNG snapshots at the phase boundary (3000) and endpoint (6000),
plus a rolling restart checkpoint. Record software, sources, schedules and jobs.

This is one bounded pilot, not a schedule/hyperparameter search. If it exposes
a numerical or data-matching defect, fix and document the defect before reading
contrasts. If the pilot is uninformative due to insufficient learning, report
that limitation and discuss a justified budget change. Any publication-level
confirmation needs fresh seeds and a protocol fixed before those outcomes.
No full confirmation or new grid is automatically launched by this protocol.
