OVERNIGHT CONTINUATION DIAGNOSTIC: FIXED PROTOCOL
Prepared before any continuation results on 5 October 2026.

Motivation
The completed original five-pair comparison is inconclusive: curriculum
minus shuffled NMSE -0.007729, 95% paired interval [-0.034687, +0.019228].
Its mean advantage was 2.45%, with three of five seeds favoring curriculum.
The clean/noisy tradeoff and context-length pattern motivate investigating
recent training conditions. This continuation is a separate exploratory
experiment; it does not replace or retest the original prespecified result.

Design and budget
Original history: curriculum or shuffled; original training seeds 0..4.
Continuation tail: high (constant context Gaussian noise SD1) or mixed
(a fixed seed-specific shuffle of evenly spaced SD0..1 levels).
All 5 x 2 x 2 = 20 branches continue for exactly 10,000 optimizer steps.
Restore model, AdamW state and episode/torch/CUDA RNG states from each
completed source checkpoint. Preserve the original Config, 10,000-step
schedule, source checkpoint bytes and original results. Continuation
outputs are separate under results/continuation_10000/seed_N/HISTORY/TAIL/.
Original effective batch2048, microbatch512, clean-target all-prefix loss,
learning rate0.0004, AdamW weight decay0.01, gradient clipping1 and BF16
operations remain fixed. Total history per final model is20,000 steps.
No early stopping and no selection of a best checkpoint.

Within each seed, every branch uses the same future clean task/input and
base Gaussian corruption draws restored from the original paired streams.
The mixed schedule uses a separate CPU generator and is identical for both
original histories within each seed. Different seeds are independent.
The high and mixed tails deliberately differ in offered noise variance:
sum(sigma squared)10,000 vs approximately3,333.5. Only history comparisons
within the same tail have matched future noise budgets. A high-vs-mixed
comparison cannot be called an equal-noise-budget ordering experiment.
Record full and final250/final1000-step noise budgets to expose finite
mixed-tail fluctuations. Use A10080GB GPUs with the same software stack;
record actual GPU variant and baseline reevaluation differences.

Measurements fixed in advance
Evaluate after0,1000,2500,5000 and10,000 added steps using the existing shared
4096-episode evaluation bank and clean targets. The main metric remains
Gaussian context noise SD2, normalized MSE averaged over k20..40.
Keep clean, lightly noisy, SD1 and other distribution/context-length
curves as secondary descriptive outcomes. Reevaluate baseline on the
newly allocated GPU; compare it to saved original metrics for provenance.
Evaluation must not advance the training RNG streams or optimizer.

Main exploratory contrast
At final10,000 define within-seed history differences
D_high = NMSE(curriculum history, high tail) - NMSE(shuffled history, high tail)
D_mixed = NMSE(curriculum history, mixed tail) - NMSE(shuffled history, mixed tail)
The main exploratory interaction is D_high - D_mixed, paired across all5seeds.
Negative interaction: curriculum relative to shuffled is favored more by
high than mixed tail. Positive interaction: the opposite direction.
Report mean, all five paired values and95% Student t interval across seeds.
Also report both D values and their changes from baseline at every fixed
timepoint. Retain all trajectories regardless of sign/significance.
Other endpoints/timepoints are secondary; no favorable-tail selection.
The planned comparison is exploratory because it was motivated by the
completed original data, and remains conditional on a fixed evaluation bank.
Intervals crossing zero are inconclusive; they do not establish equivalence.
No practically meaningful equivalence margin has been specified.

Interpretation and decision
If history gaps diminish under both common endings, the result is consistent
with recent training conditions and/or additional convergence accounting for
some of the original pattern. This does not uniquely establish recency.
If gaps persist, ordering history survives these particular interventions;
this does not uniquely establish a gradual-curriculum teaching mechanism.
An interaction would show that the effect of ending distribution depends
on original history. High/mixed tails bundle noise intensity/distribution.
Compare fixed evaluations at5000 and10,000 for stability, while retaining
the final10,000 comparison regardless of stability. Five seeds provide
limited precision and t inference assumes roughly normal paired differences.
Any clear pattern would motivate a separately planned replication at another
problem size and/or a bridge under verified original paper/code settings.
If the diagnostic stays unclear, preserve the inconclusive result and
reassess further compute; do not add seeds until significance appears.
