IDUN H1 follow-up and exploratory checkpoint diagnostics
Fixed before submission: 2026-10-06. Seeds 0,1,2,3,4.

Purpose
The completed original curriculum/shuffled comparison (H2) and its continuation
interaction remain unchanged. This follow-up tests noise exposure versus clean
training (H1) and explores prediction behavior. It does not establish an internal
algorithm or guarantee a publishable positive result.

Clean-only training
Train five models from scratch for 20,000 updates, retaining immutable model,
AdamW, schedule and RNG snapshots at 10,000 and 20,000 updates. The unchanged
run_experiment.py trainer, full BF16 configuration, initialization seeds,
CPU task/input/corruption generators and microbatch draw order are reused.
Only context corruption changes: sigma=0 throughout. Architecture, optimizer,
constant learning rate, clipping, effective batch 2048, microbatch 512, clean
all-prefix target loss, dimension 20 and 41 prediction positions are identical.
Noise draws are still consumed, preserving paired episode streams. No early
stopping, schedule search or extra curriculum variants. Resume preserves AdamW
and training RNG and refuses changed protocol, source or code.

Primary H1 endpoint
At 20,000 total updates, compare existing shuffled+mixed models to clean-only
models, per seed: D = noisy NMSE - clean-only NMSE. Negative D favors noise
exposure. Gaussian context noise SD=2, clean-target MSE divided by dimension 20,
averaged over k=20..40, on the unchanged shared 4,096-episode bank seed 81973.
Report all five paired differences, mean difference and two-sided 95% Student
t interval (df=4). This is a new comparison using completed noisy arms; it is
not an independent replication of those arms. Uncertainty is over training
seeds, conditional on this test bank. No equivalence or superiority claim if
the interval includes zero. Secondary: corresponding 10,000-step shuffled vs
clean-only difference, other noise conditions, k curves, and clean-performance
cost. All secondary intervals are descriptive, unadjusted for multiplicity.
The original shuffled schedule and its separately shuffled mixed tail have
the same [0,1] SD multiset in each 10k phase. These are the stationary noisy
comparisons; they are not identical to one new shuffle spanning 20k updates.

Exploratory checkpoint diagnostics
Inspect all 10 original 10k models, 20 final 20k continuation models, and the
10 new clean snapshots. Do not infer availability of intermediate weights.
Use fresh Gaussian episode banks: calibration seed 921731 (512 episodes),
held-out seed 921733 (4,096 episodes), distinct from bank 81973. Conditions
SD=0,0.5,1,2; k=20..40. All models share the same banks.
Ridge prior w~N(0,I), convention beta=(X'X+lambda I)^(-1)X'y.
Fixed references: minimum-norm OLS, lambda=1/3,1,4; known-SD Bayes ridge uses
lambda=sigma^2 and is explicitly privileged. It is not an information-matched
training baseline. No comparison assumes the model knows the test SD.
Ridge-fit grid: zero, 0.25,1/3,1,4, plus 41 log-spaced positive penalties
from 1e-4 to 100. Select one penalty per model and condition minimizing
prediction discrepancy across calibration episodes and k=20..40. Selection
matches transformer predictions, not query targets. Held-out target errors
and prediction discrepancies are then reported per k and averaged over late
contexts. Selection is exploratory, not a fitted mechanism proof.
An empirical Bayes reference selects a positive grid penalty separately for
each context by maximizing the Gaussian marginal likelihood of preceding
context labels under the known task prior. It sees preceding X and y only,
never the query target or true test SD. Its grid imposes a numerical floor
1e-4 and ceiling 100; assumptions and limitations must accompany reporting.
OLS at k=dimension with noisy labels has heavy-tailed/infinite-mean risk in
ideal Gaussian settings; report finite-bank values without implying stability.

Symmetry checks
On the first 512 held-out episodes, compare original predictions to one fixed
orthogonal rotation of all input vectors (QR seed 921743, labels unchanged),
and jointly permute preceding input-label pairs independently per episode
and k (seed 921739+k), retaining the same query vector. Query label is zero
and inaccessible. Report prediction changes and task errors. Ridge references
are mathematically invariant; meaningful neural sensitivity would undermine
a literal ridge description. Approximate agreement alone is not mechanistic
proof. No exhaustive transformation search or cherry-picking.

Validation and reporting
Before full GPU runs, test causal references against direct linear algebra,
known-SD Bayes reference, permutation/rotation invariance, context-only marginal
likelihood, reproducible disjoint calibration/test banks, unchanged trainer
agreement, paired episode streams, interruption/resume and retained snapshots.
Use separate Slurm jobs with afterok dependencies, immutable source hashes and
recorded submission manifest. GPU production runs use the verified prior CUDA
environment, not login-node training. Automatic final summary requires all
controls and all 40 checkpoint diagnostic records. Missing/failed work cannot
be silently dropped. Report primary/secondary distinctions and all seeds.
Keep conclusions about H1, H2, finite-budget effects and ridge-like predictions
separate. No additional compute or seed extension chosen to chase significance.
