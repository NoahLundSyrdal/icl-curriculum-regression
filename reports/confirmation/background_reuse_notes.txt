L(theta,t) = E[(1/|K_t|) sum_{k in K_t} (M_theta(P^k) - y_{k+1})^2].
P^k contains k demonstrations and the next query input. Causal attention hides its target. K_t has 2d+1 prediction positions for an active dimension d, reaching 101 at d=50. SUGGESTED FRAMING
Training Curricula for In-Context Linear Regression: A Clean-Accuracy and Noise-Robustness Trade-off

SUGGESTED BACKGROUND PARAGRAPH
In-context learning lets a trained model predict outputs for a new task from demonstrations supplied at inference, without updating its parameters. Synthetic linear regression provides a controlled setting for studying how this capability is acquired. Garg et al. (2022) established dimension/context curriculum benefits, and Liu et al. (2025) studied curricula, task complexity and architecture in synthetic ICL. Shin and Alvarez-Melis (2026) distinguish ordering from cumulative difficulty exposure with a static exposure-matched control across a broad task suite. Our follow-up compares a dimension/context curriculum against the exact same realized batches, targets, masks and scales reshuffled in time. We ask how that ordering changes clean acquisition and robustness to noisy demonstrations. The experiments show a clean/noisy trade-off; whether this reflects weaker implicit regularization is a hypothesis for further testing.

RELATED WORK AND SCOPE
Shin and Alvarez-Melis is the closest ordering-control work. Its static control matches cumulative exposure by difficulty level, not the identical finite realized batch multiset. It also includes a real-data supervised-fine-tuning appendix; avoid describing the whole paper as exclusively symbolic. Our different control and regression/noise setting are candidate contributions, not a verified first-in-literature claim. Liu et al. (2025) here means Binwen Liu and colleagues' synthetic-ICL architecture study, not a similarly named prompt-ordering citation. Do not claim generic curriculum benefits for ICL are new or that this confirms all curricula, other architectures, pretrained LLMs, or general tasks. The ridge mechanism is not established by a curve fit alone.
